myMowing Operations Planner
Workload = summed EST minutes from Zenkit (these already include each job's driving allowance, so travel is not added again). Each vehicle is assessed on its own crew, jobs, specialist cover and shift window; status is Red if any vehicle can't complete its work, Amber if tight. The What-if planner only lets you allocate staff who are genuinely rostered/available in Deputy — it never invents people or moves jobs off their date.
