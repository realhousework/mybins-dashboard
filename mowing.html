myMowing Operations Planner
Prototype on a live snapshot of your Zenkit jobs + Deputy roster. Workload = summed EST (one-person-equivalent) minutes of each day's dated jobs; capacity = rostered person-hours. Two-person crews finish faster than this (driving isn't halved), so a green here is conservative. Travel is an area-based estimate (depot + hops between Manly / North Shore / Warringah-Pittwater), not live GPS. Days you haven't dated yet show as "not scheduled".
